Collect useful words and phrases without breaking your reading flow, then return to organize and learn them later.

Highlight text on any webpage Alt/⌥ ⇧ P
  1. 01

    Capture in place

    Highlight a phrase, use the shortcut, or choose Save to Phraselet from the context menu. Selections over 500 characters are shortened at the nearest word boundary.

  2. 02

    Organize your library

    Search, add notes and tags, archive phrases you want to keep out of the way, or select several phrases for bulk organization.

  3. 03

    Keep control

    Your library stays on this device, and you can export or import it whenever you want a portable backup.

Your data stays local

Saved phrases, nearby context, page titles, URLs, notes, and tags are stored in Chrome on this device. Phraselet accesses a page only when you explicitly save a selection and does not send your library to a third party.
