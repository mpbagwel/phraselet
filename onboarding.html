Your data and AI

Saved phrases, nearby context, page titles, URLs, notes, tags, and your optional API key are stored in Chrome on this device. Phraselet accesses a page only when you save a selection. When AI explanations are enabled, the phrase, nearby context, page title, and site hostname are sent to OpenAI without asking OpenAI to retain the generated response.
